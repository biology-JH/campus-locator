ROOMFINDER COMPLETE MIGRATION GUIDE
====================================

Purpose
-------
Continue and migrate the existing Roomfinder classroom vacancy and occupancy website. Do not rebuild it from scratch. Preserve the React / Express / tRPC / Drizzle architecture, current interface, timetable data, admin upload workflow, tests, and authoritative teacher-resolution logic.

Application behavior
--------------------
- Staff select Week A or Week B, Monday to Friday, and Mentor Group or Lesson 1 through Lesson 7 to find completely free classrooms.
- Staff can search a room and view its complete Week A and Week B schedule.
- Show Free and Occupied status clearly and keep the interface usable on mobile.
- Exclude GLC, Auditorium, LT1, LT2, GLC glassroom, A603, and A708 from room search, free-room results, and master schedules.
- Keep the designated Lab-room labels and the secure /admin timetable upload workflow, including validation, progress, exact success messaging, transactional replacement, and parse-error protection.
- Preserve the current database migrations and checked-in timetable export. Do not overwrite production/shared data casually.

Teacher-resolution precedence
-----------------------------
1. Apply an exact entry from server/data/manual-staff-overrides.json only when room, week, day, period, and the full occupant/source text all match.
2. Apply explicit exclusions and contextual rules in server/staff-resolution.ts.
3. Apply grade-and-subject rules in server/data/teacher-subject-rules.json.
4. Use server/data/teacher-abbreviations.json as the complete roster and abbreviation fallback.
5. Never guess between duplicate initials. Preserve unresolved conflicts unless the user gives a confirmed choice.
6. The final two alphabetic tokens in a source label are teacher initials, not necessarily the middle portion of a name.
